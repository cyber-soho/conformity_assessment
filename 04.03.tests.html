Facts to keep in mind

An asset inventory is evidence only if it is complete (both reconciliation directions), owned (no empty owner cell), classified (level, PI / CHD flags, impact) and current (reviewed within the cadence, drift ticketed). The requirements behind it: CIS 1.1 / 2.1 / 3.2, ISO/IEC 27001 A.5.9 / 5.12, NIST CSF 2.0 ID.AM, PCI DSS 12.5.1 / 9.5.1.1, and the privacy laws through the PI flag.
